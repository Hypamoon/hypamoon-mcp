# Security

## Report a vulnerability

Email **security@hypamoon.com**. Describe the issue, the steps to reproduce it, the affected address and what an attacker could do with it. You can write in English or Dutch.

Please read our policy first: https://hypamoon.com/security. Our contact details are also in https://hypamoon.com/.well-known/security.txt.

In short:

- **In scope:** our own services, including the MCP server at `https://api.hypamoon.com/api/v1/mcp` (the policy has the full list), and flaws in the platform behind sites and apps on hypamoon.app, such as one project reaching the data of another.
- **Do not test without written permission.** No automated scanners on production, no denial of service, no social engineering, and no access to other people's data. Use only accounts and projects you created yourself.
- **Tell us first** and give us reasonable time to fix it before you share anything.
- **No bug bounty.** We do not pay for reports and do not buy unsolicited audits.

A real report in good faith that follows these rules gets our attention. We aim to confirm receipt within 5 business days, and we will not take legal action against you over it.

## Report abuse

Phishing, fraud, spam or other misuse of a site, number or message on Hypamoon is not a vulnerability. Report it at https://hypamoon.com/abuse or email abuse@hypamoon.com.
